## What passes

- **Spotlight fade.** Measured on D2 now s2: the box goes `hidden` within 1 frame, reaches opacity 0 at 113 ms and stays hidden through the resize. It returns at the final geometry (702 by 181) and is fully in about 100 ms later. It never slides or stretches.
- **Spotlight radius.** Radii follow the target on D1 (popover button 6, dialog 16), D3 (textarea), D6 (input, capture card 12), F1 (rule 12), D5 and F2 drawers (12), and table rows and timeline items (0). Rings are concentric.
- **Backgrounds.** All 21 files are distinct, smooth multi-hue fields with grain (`review/bg-thumbs.png`). Title, opening and closing slides each have their own file. Every section's intro, used-to and now pages share one file, and neighbouring sections differ. The grain overlay is present at 0.10 overlay with a 256 px tile at scale 1.
- **Wrappers.** The wrappers are specific to each demo and pass the two-second test: order queue (D1), storefront (D2, D5, F2), case desk with the order 418 thread (D3), app chrome only on D4, product editor (D6), watch list, advisory feed, terminal, CI report and draft PR. The v4 empty-pane shell is gone. Glass tiers are consistent: quiet context and one raised shown block.
- **Code panel.** Every walk page splits 50/50 (frame 880 at x 64, code panel 880 at x 976). Nothing misaligns at the split.
- **Closing slide.** It shows "Bermon Painter", "Head of DXP and Commerce, Slalom" and "linkedin.com/in/bermonpainter" with the LinkedIn mark. The QR is navy on a solid white card, 518 px with 14 px modules, encoding `https://www.linkedin.com/in/bermonpainter`, with a "Scan to open LinkedIn" caption. It is legible and on-brand. The phone scan from the back of the room is still owed in M7 and M8.
- **Opening and agenda slides.** Title, mandate, three reactions, two perspectives, open web and what we'll cover, plus what changed and people to follow, are native glass layouts on their own backgrounds. They use one type scale, footer pills and good contrast (white on navy 0.72, navy on white 0.72).
- **Writing rules.** There are no em or en dashes in slide, section or wrapper copy. Headings are in sentence case, and slide CSS has no uppercase or tracking. The F3 now chip switching from "Recorded" to "Example session" is intended (`demos/07-package-workflow/build.ts`) and honest.
