## Writing rules to apply (Slalom writing guide, as stated in the skill)

The guide's reference folder was not available on this machine, so these are the rules the skill states directly:

- No em dashes or en dashes in any copy.
- Sentence-case headings and labels. Headings are short plain labels.
- One claim per sentence, stated once. Plain professional verbs; no idioms or figurative verbs.
- Stop each sentence when its point lands; no trailing clauses bolted on.
- "X, not Y" at most once, never in a heading.
- Address the reader directly. No buzzwords, no hype, no exclamation points.
- Beginner reading level: short sentences (about 15 words), common words, define a term the first time.
