## What passes

- **Slides.** Title, mandate and agenda match spec 6.2 to the pixel (`slideprobe.mjs`). The mandate's six questions are short and in sentence case, with curly apostrophes.
- **D1 hole mask.** The menu item paints over the toast ring. At s2 the dialog and menu rings are cut correctly where layers stack.
- **D2.** No card crosses the footer at any width (560 and 640) on either page. The callout and rings use the card's 8 px radius.
- **D3.** Text fits at s2 on both pages. The s5 "field sizing off" scroll is intended and reads as the contrast.
- **D4 detail.** The header with a status chip, the items table with totals, and the customer, ship-to and timeline card are believable and well aligned. Order 418 matches D3's side card (Base layer, 3, $162). The 60/40 split gives the code panel 44 columns with no wrapping.
- **D6.** The photo swaps with the posted color on both builds. Option thumbnails are 48 by 36 with a 4 px radius and line up in the picker and in `selectedcontent`. The Firefox step is now a clean card, not the old poster.
- **F2.** The product card is clean: photo tile, title, price, copy, stock chip. The price is $129, matching D2 and D5 (v5 M9 fixed). Log rows are mono 14 and newest first.
- **Copy.** There are no em or en dashes in changed content, fixtures or wrappers. Every on-screen sentence I checked has 15 words or fewer.
